# SDK68 paired source composition

This unregistered composition adds paired primary Squall entry, all four primary
animations, and the two ground physics callbacks; complete secondary Squall
359/360 phases and recruitment; and complete non-null secondary Belay
361/362/364/365 phases, catch/launch/availability and live endpoint observations.
Existing accepted SDK66 solo and Ice Shot/Blizzard files remain unchanged and
are composed directly. This does not activate an independent paired package.

The console matrix passes **4,240 original/generated pairs, 288,389 checks and
6 separate authored safety cases**. There are 92 extracted complete bodies,
including the candidate target-bone boundary. Popo imports 48 retail parameter
declarations plus 6 owned defaults; Nana imports 12 plus 8 owned defaults.
Immutable SDK66 is the complete original-C
reference; SDK68 supplies the compiler, ABI, actual generation registry,
relations operations and target-bone observation. Retail PlPp/PlNn DAT values
become named parameters with serialized offsets and bits in provenance.json.
Offsets are fixture import facts; scripts use named parameters and owned state.

Run from the repository, choosing a new output directory:

```powershell
python tests/native/ice_climbers_linked/paired_run.py --root . --baseline .local/baseline68 --reference .local/baseline66 --output A:/FreakMelee-test-archives/ice-paired68-final --iso "C:/gayms/freak-melee/Super Smash Bros. Melee (USA) (v1.02).iso"
```

The runner uses frozen script-c and explicit frozen Clang/SDK for both actual
generated DLLs. It retains commands, complete original bodies and body/source
hashes, real DAT bytes, parameter provenance, input/output hashes and run.log
in result.json. No game, audio, global build or registration is involved.

## Comparison boundaries

The complete extracted original bodies retain their original symbols and call
order, including the original Nana cleanup entry. Shared gravity, acceleration,
clamping, ground-to-self conversion, mpCopyCollData and hitlag release are real
source bodies. Native callback lifetime and fighter relation units are linked
directly; the bounded BonePosition operation is extracted intact from SDK68.

Family member topology, typed export routing, current-motion observation,
motion/animation entry and completion, floor/air probes, ground friction,
world-position transforms and root rotations are controlled call boundaries.
Traces compare ordered arguments, command/callback publication and the two
fighters' resulting scalar/motion/collision state. The fixture does not prove
the lower animation, stage geometry, physics matrix or generic target-dispatch
implementation. Collision storage starts explicitly zeroed before field-copy
comparison; no undefined padding is used as behavioral evidence.

The six safety cases separately test missing peers, source retirement/recycling
during a world-position query, and stale linked generation retirement/recycling.
They have no defined original raw-pointer parity contract and are not counted
as healthy stock pairs. Full-width high-bit creation IDs are retained.

`int(action.category)` is a lossless conversion of the four-bit category in
SDK68; the existing compiler classifier treats that scalar conservatively as
Float. No fighter-specific native coercion is introduced.

## Remaining closure

Primary Belay 347..349/352..354 entry/rope scheduling and the full Gumstrings
article solver/render/resource lifecycle remain unported here. Primary Squall
collision callbacks and the private-union Squall effect updater transport are
also excluded. The source ceiling comparison `(flags & CeilingMask) == 1`
must be preserved or reviewed as a separate semantic decision, not silently
changed to a nonzero predicate. Script cleanup composition exists, but complete
rope removal/cleanup is not exercised with a live rope instance.

Early follower CPU/history initialization, paired package/rig publication and
whole lifecycle acceptance belong to the separate shared foundation. This
receipt does not claim those, independent activation, or gameplay parity.
Prior diagnostic directories ice-paired68-first through ice-paired68-seventh
remain available; their preparation/link-binding failures are not successes.
