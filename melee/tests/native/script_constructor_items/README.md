This silent console cohort executes the source-exact ordinary Item teardown and
Fighter userdata Unload bodies with actual GObj/Fighter pools and actual authored
Fighter/Article VM callbacks. The factory proof is the unchanged common ownership
and admission prefix; model loading, Item attribute/ECB initialization and whole
Fighter construction are outside that boundary. Camera, effect, status, sound,
rig disposal and Item payload allocation are explicit controlled leaves.

Failed construction is marked on its actual Fighter payload. The cleanup uses
the immutable creator GObj pool stamp and current damage owner, independent of
the logical life generation. Transferred/unowned and unrelated Items survive.
Retiring constructors cannot publish further original-created children through
the common factory, including an Item-parent path. This is failure-only cleanup,
not a general transaction rollback or a changed normal Fighter unload policy.

The corpus covers generation-zero Load products and post-NewLife products,
normal complete-Fighter survivors, native and interpreted Item Destroyed hooks,
Remove/Destroyed factory refusal, source Item creator inheritance, null creator,
old-source address reuse, a peer transferred during Destroyed and a peer whose
physical allocation is replaced during Destroyed. Load also retires its owner
directly, or faults through an actual interpreted division by zero after its
child is created. Those paths exercise the real actor/VM and Unload body without
claiming the complete Fighter_Create constructor.

Native userdata validity follows the actual GObj registry invariant. This proof
does not claim arbitrary invalid userdata, unowned food/effect rollback, live
gameplay acceptance, or any audio/GPU/device operation. Resource graph teardown
has a separate genuine graph comparison in script_item_resources.
