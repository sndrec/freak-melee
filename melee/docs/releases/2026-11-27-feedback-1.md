## Workshop

- The Add Event menu groups events by purpose. The sound picker lets you search loaded Melee sound banks and fighter clips, preview sounds, and assign one to an event.
- Open an action's behavior script directly from the Action Editor. Add an **Execute Script** event to run a fighter function at a specific frame.
- **Spawn Effect** events appear as you scrub the action timeline. Effects advance with the playhead and reset when you switch actions.
- Fighter scripts can take one of their own stocks with `damage.quiet_ko()`. Normal scoring and respawn timing still apply, with a quiet disappearance in place.

## Practice and stage select

- Choose Sandbag or an installed fighter as your practice opponent. Open **Practice opponent** on character select, or press **Z** from the training menu. Changing the opponent during practice returns to character select.
- The training menu stays available while you wait in a lobby. Spawn items and adjust opponent damage as practice continues. **START** toggles readiness and cancels the countdown if you unready.
- Battlefield, Final Destination, and Dream Land now show the matching stage thumbnails.

Restart through the launcher to update before playing online together.
