## Menus and controls

- Reorganized Settings into Melee-style Video, Audio, Game, Online and Controls menus. Controller profiles, button bindings and calibration now live in Controls, alongside one shared setting for the players on this PC.
- Added separate master, effects and music volume controls.
- Gave News its own wider reader and history page. The ticker now scrolls within its frame with fading edges.
- Announcements now use Melee's original unlock notification graphics, dim the screen behind them, and wait two seconds before accepting dismissal.
- Improved menu lettering, button alignment, chat layout and navigation. Lobby and queue status remain visible while browsing the hub, with controls to return or leave.
- Restored the original proportions and presentation of CSS player cards. Empty seats show N/A, only configured local players have hands, and lobby controls sit below the taller chat area.
- Fixed long feedback notes scrolling and keeping the text cursor visible while editing.

## Online play

- In 1v1 Quick Play, multiple local players can choose who plays by placing their token. The most recently placed token takes the local seat and clears its previous readiness.
- Quick Play can search from character select before entering practice. Practice shows matchmaking status and incoming chat; START pauses and unreadies, then resumes and readies on the next press. The normal paused match-exit combination returns to character select.
- Player-created lobbies start a three-second countdown when every active fighter is ready. Waiting players and spectators do not block it. Lobby rules, item settings and random-stage choices are shared with everyone before the match starts.
- Added the remaining stock stage-selection policies alongside Loser: Anyone, Random, Ordered and Turns.
- Players watching stage selection can now see the chooser's cursor, hovered stage and identity. Automatic stage picks go straight to loading without briefly showing stage select.
- Fixed rollback stopping character voices before their sounds finish.

## Workshop

- Separated Duplicate from Clone Vanilla Fighter. Importing a fighter package creates a new editable draft; importing a model changes the current draft.
- Moved authoring commands out of Attributes. Save, Build & install and Test Character are easier to reach, and the editor header uses two rows at narrow window widths.
- Portrait editing supports choosing an animation and frame, with separate framing for the CSS tile and player card, plus a properly sized player-card preview. Fixed portrait shadows to render black.
- New vanilla fighter clones include their original stock icons and HUD emblems. Both images can be replaced through PNG import in the editor's Fighter menu, including separate images for paired-fighter members.

## Gameplay and rendering

- Fixed temporary move-created wind persisting in practice and making Ganondorf's cape keep flailing after Warlock Punch. Corrected bone collision storage used by fighters and items.
- Restored fighter shadows in the practice room and fixed texture-copy edges at scaled window sizes.
- Restored white damage numbers at 0%.
