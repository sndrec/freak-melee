## Lobbies

- Claim an open player card or give up your own directly on character select. The screen shows the lobby's configured number of fighters, and the cards keep their original Melee proportions.
- See other players' hands, including spectators, on character select. Held tokens are translucent so you can tell them apart from your own.
- In Winner Stays and Winner Out lobbies, place your token on a fighter to get in line. Open spots go to the players who have waited longest since their last match, with ties picked at random.
- Use the side arrows in Rules to reach Lobby Settings. Left and right cycle through settings with the same sliding values as Melee's Rules menu.
- Finished matches return you to the same lobby's character select.
- Friends' profiles put the lobby invitation above Remove Friend.

## Practice and menus

- Choose a stage every time you enter local practice from character select. Practice uses the original Training menu without the online status banner. START toggles the menu once per press, and holding a direction repeats at the original rate.
- Press B or Escape on a main hub tab to open a quit confirmation.
- The global chatroom loads the latest 16 messages when you connect. It keeps receiving messages while you play and preserves the messages it has already loaded when reconnecting.
- Announcements go to players who had the game open when they were sent. If you're in a match, the announcement waits for an eligible screen.

This update changes online lobby communication. Restart through the launcher to update before joining or hosting a lobby.
