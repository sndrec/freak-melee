## Online play

- The game resends missing controller inputs sooner and keeps repairing older gaps as play continues. In a simulated 80 ms round-trip match, both players recovered from a 12-frame interruption that timed out on the previous build.
- Fighter transfers resend the missing pieces of a package. A 6 MiB transfer on the same simulated 80 ms connection went from 0.155 to 1.168 MiB/s. During a match, player inputs take priority over downloads to spectators.
- Other players' hands move more smoothly on character select and stage select. Player-created lobbies keep each card in its assigned slot. Pressing Back in Rules returns directly to character select when you opened Rules from there.
- A pair of players can reconnect a dropped gameplay connection while the other lobby connections stay open. The lobby browser shows rooms for your build, hides rooms that have stopped responding and shows them again when a player reconnects.
- Lobby matches start with the stage, rules and fighters everyone readied with. If players report different results, the lobby keeps them in their current seats.

## Fighters and Workshop

- Fighter packages can be up to 12 MiB, or 32 MiB before compression. The Workshop uses lossless compression when needed. Rebuild oversized fighters from their Workshop drafts before sharing them. If a rebuild changes the fighter's package ID, rebuild its outfits too.
- Online play keeps up to 32 automatically downloaded fighters and outfits. When the cache fills, it replaces the oldest download that's free. Fighters and outfits you're using stay available, and accepted gifts stay in your collection.
- The game checks models, animations, hitboxes, sounds and scripts before loading shared fighters. Costume checks catch missing bones and incompatible outfits before a match. A script that uses up its callback's instruction budget stops with a diagnostic.
- Workshop saves and exports preserve existing files if an operation fails. Animation previews refresh when the animation changes.

## Feedback and updates

- F2 feedback keeps the screenshot and game state from the moment you pressed the key. Retrying a submission sends that same capture and your confirmed notes.
- The launcher now checks a publisher signature before installing an update. Both players should restart through the launcher to get this build before playing online together.
