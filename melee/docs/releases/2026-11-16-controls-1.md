## Controls

- Added a controller picker under **Settings → Controls → Player profiles & bindings**. Choose a player slot, then press **Controller** to assign a connected controller without repeating first startup.
- The picker shows each controller's current player slot and updates as devices connect or disconnect.
- Selecting a controller from another occupied slot swaps the two controllers. Each slot keeps its assigned profile and controls.
- Controller assignments are remembered across launches. **No controller** clears the selected slot's device assignment.
- Made **Use defaults** the same size as Reset and Delete, with the new Controller button immediately to its left.
